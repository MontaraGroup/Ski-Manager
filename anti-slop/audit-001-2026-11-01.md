# antislop audit 001, ski-manager.net

Date: 2026-11-01
Mode: AFTER (audit, nothing changed yet)
Rules: antislop core + antislop-ui
Scope: live code (`origin/main` at `3f63f52`: `app/Views`, `public/css/input.css`, player-facing strings in controllers and the game tick), plus the public pages on https://ski-manager.net at 375 px and desktop, both themes.
Not covered: signed-in pages in a browser. I did not sign in to production; signed-in screens were reviewed from source only.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

Direction note (R-37): the site has no `DESIGN.md`. Fixes to contrast, states, copy and markup don't need one. Anything that changes the palette or the look (items 10 to 16) would be a draft without direction until a `DESIGN.md` exists or you give a direction.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **Home page breaks in the light theme** (R-34, R-25). `app/Views/home.php` hardcodes dark-theme colors. With the Winter Alpine theme on, the brand name measures 1.04:1, the "Season 2 is open", "How a season goes" and "Recently built" headings measure 1.03:1 to 1.12:1, and the Season 1 leaderboard rows measure 1.03:1. They are effectively invisible. The theme toggle is in the navbar, so any player using light mode sees this.

2. **Primary button text fails contrast in the dark theme** (R-25). Every `btn-primary` measures 3.25:1 (white text on `--p: oklch(62% 0.18 245)`); 4.5:1 is needed. This covers "Play free", which appears 3 times on the home page, and every primary action in the game.

3. **Muted text below AA in both themes** (R-25). Dark theme: footer text, table headers, dates and the Mapsynergy credit measure 3.71 to 4.32:1. Light theme: the same muted text measures 3.34 to 3.63:1. The cause is `text-base-content/40` to `/60` opacity utilities used for secondary text across the site.

4. **Hover feedback removed site-wide** (R-39). `public/css/input.css:1324-1344` ("REMOVE ALL HOVER EFFECTS ACROSS SITE") sets `transition: none !important` on everything and strips hover from links (`a:hover` underline off), table rows and cards. Buttons keep their background change; links and clickable rows have no hover state left.

5. **Endless animations with no pause control** (R-39, R-19).
   - `animate-pulse` loops forever on: the notifications page bell (`notifications/index.php:9`), "Live" and "Active" tournament badges (`tournaments/index.php:65,101`), the night skiing "LIVE" badge (`nightskiing/index.php:42`), and the "More" bonus dot in the mobile dock (`layouts/main.php:1004`).
   - `animate-ping` loops on the roadmap (`roadmap/index.php:173`).
   - A reduced-motion override exists, but anything moving longer than 5 seconds also needs to stop or have a pause control. The bell marks no state at all.

6. **Night skiing "LIVE" is driven by the server clock, not the game** (R-38). `nightskiing/index.php:37-42` says "Night skiing is active now" and shows a pulsing LIVE badge between 17:00 and 07:00 server time. Night skiing is settled once a day by the tick, so nothing is live, and players in other time zones see the wrong state.

7. **Tap targets under 44 px** (R-03), measured at 375 px:
   - `.btn` is 40 px tall (`min-height: 2.5rem`).
   - `btn-sm` is 32 px, which includes the navbar "Log in" and "Play free" buttons and the Discord and GitHub footer icons.
   - Footer links are 17 px tall, and inline links such as "Sign in" and "Full leaderboard" are 17 to 20 px.
   - In all, 34 controls on the home page alone are under 44 px.

8. **Em dashes in UI text** (R-02).
   - 15 in views: `maintenance.php:7,127`; `layouts/main.php:536,558,562,566,578` (impersonation bar and the stat-pill tooltips); `finances/index.php:9`; `legal/privacy.php:61-64`; empty-cell markers in `home.php:132`, `grooming/index.php:102` and `resort_analysis/view.php:68`.
   - 17 in strings players see:
     - flash messages: `Settings.php:60`
     - activity log: `Morale.php:97`; `GameTick.php:244,605,659,793`
     - notifications: `Resort.php:151`
     - event descriptions: `Tournaments.php:20,21`

9. **Accessibility gaps** (R-39, R-40).
   - **Render-blocking script:** the Sentry bundle (`browser.sentry-cdn.com/8.38.0/bundle.tracing.replay.min.js`) is loaded in `<head>` with neither `defer` nor `async`, so it blocks rendering of every page.
   - **Missing names:** the back-to-top button (`#backToTop`) has no accessible name. The Discord and GitHub footer links are named only by `title`, and their icons aren't `aria-hidden`.
   - **Landmarks:** the page has no `<header>` landmark.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

10. **Same gradient card on every building page, colored by page, not by meaning** (R-01, R-14).
    - Each building page opens with the same tinted gradient card, a different color per page:
      - `buildings/hotel.php:29`: primary
      - `rental.php:26`: info
      - `restaurant.php:30`: warning
      - `transportation.php:27`: accent
      - `retail.php:27`: secondary
      - `ski_patrol.php:33`: error
    - The same pattern repeats in `vip_guests/index.php:22`, `scenic/index.php:18` (primary to secondary) and `parking/index.php:111`.
    - The colors carry no state. Error red on the ski patrol page reads as a warning.

11. **Night skiing header** (R-01, R-12, R-13). `nightskiing/index.php:33` uses a slate-to-indigo gradient card with `shadow-xl`, a style used nowhere else in the game, plus the pulsing badge from items 5 and 6.

12. **Colored glow on toasts** (R-13). `layouts/main.php:925-928` gives each toast a colored glow shadow by icon: blue, cyan, fuchsia for trophies, red for warnings. Every toast glows, so glow marks nothing.

13. **Emoji as decoration** (R-04).
    - Flash messages and notifications: `Demo.php:75,375`; `Alliances.php:267,443,498,569,654`.
    - Map editor:
      - Difficulty options in `resort_map/index.php:470-473,730-731`. These are inconsistent: the second list uses a square 🟦 where the first uses a circle 🔵.
      - The "🧲 Snapped" label at `resort_map/index.php:1855`.
    - Leaderboard medals: `leaderboard/index.php:5`.
    - Admin broadcast icon options: `admin/broadcast.php:30-34`.

14. **Uppercase wide-tracked labels** (R-06). 31 uses across 13 views, for example "SNOW REPORT" on the home page, plus `vote/index.php:187-195`, `settings/index.php`, `resort/index.php` and the maintenance countdown. Several are 10 to 11 px, which also hurts readability.

15. **Blur beyond the dose cap** (R-10).
    - Every page: the navbar (`input.css:1222`), the mobile dock (`input.css:1319`) and the modal backdrop (`input.css:1086`). That's three surfaces against a cap of 1 to 2.
    - `maintenance.php` adds 6 blurred panels, 5 gradients and glow on top.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

16. **Palette wider than 2 to 3 colors plus 1 accent** (R-29).
    - Both themes ship primary blue, a purple secondary and an accent: light grey in dark mode, magenta in light mode.
    - The map editor adds its own purple (`#c084fc`).
    - Secondary and accent appear mostly as decoration (items 10 and 12), not at a key moment.

17. **No design direction on record** (R-37, R-20, R-06). There is no `DESIGN.md`. Inter is used with no written reason, and dials (ENERGY, RHYTHM, MOTION) were never set.

18. **Wrong currency in the Génépis shop** (R-38 copy accuracy). "Cash Injection: Receive $100,000 cash immediately" (`Genepis.php:27`), while the game is in euros everywhere else.

19. **Brand repeated in the home page title** (copy). The browser tab reads "Ski Manager - Free Online Ski Resort Tycoon Game - Ski Manager": `home.php:2` includes the brand and the layout appends it again.

20. **Dead file** (C-3). `app/Views/dashboard/index.bak.php` is unused and carries its own gradient and glow styles.

## Passed

- No fake statistics or testimonials. The home page numbers (454 players, 993 runs, the richest resort) are live data.
- The FAQ questions are specific to the game.
- No horizontal overflow at 375 px on the home page.
- Images have `alt` and dimensions.
- Visible focus ring via `*:focus-visible`.
- The skip link is present.
- Viewport zoom is not blocked.
- There is one `h1`.
- Every footer link resolves (200, or a redirect to sign-in for game pages).
